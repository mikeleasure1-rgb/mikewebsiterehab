Before you call

Have these ready

Photo ID

A driver's license or state ID. Expect a careful locksmith to check it before opening anything.

Proof it's yours

Home: a lease, deed or utility bill with the address. Car: registration or title. Business: proof you're authorized.

Car details

Make, model and year, and whether it has a push-button start, a fob, or a regular key.

ID locked inside? Say so when you call. Any locksmith: ask for the total price before work starts.
